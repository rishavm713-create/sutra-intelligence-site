# Roadmap

[← Back to README](../README.md)

Direction, not promises. No dates are given and none should be inferred. Items may change or be dropped.

## Now: under active development
- Model and tool selection across providers
- Reducing repeated operations
- Support for more than one AI product
- Structured outcomes and feedback

## Next: real-world validation
The most important step. Without it nothing else matters.
- Define fair real-world tasks and a baseline of using models directly
- Measure reliability, waste, cost and human effort honestly
- Publish results, including negative ones, only once they exist

## Later: proposed
- Broader evidence handling
- Clearer reporting of why a result is or is not trusted
- Wider tool coverage
- Ways for others to evaluate the work under agreed terms (not open source; any such arrangement would need explicit written permission)

## What is not planned
- Publishing internal source, rules or research in this repository
- Claiming improvements before they are measured

## Status

Prototype. Core hypothesis unproven.
