अहं ब्रह्मास्मि
Ahaṃ Brahmāsmi
“I am Brahman” — Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 1.4.10

Śiva ▲ — the witness

Stillness, principle and the fixed objective: the part that knows what must be true before anything is called done.

Śakti ▽ — the force

Energy, action and recovery: the part that plans, executes and adapts, always held inside the aim set by Śiva.
