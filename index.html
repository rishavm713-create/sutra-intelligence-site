01 · Philosophy and vision

AI you can trust to finish.
And prove it did.

The problem: AI agents drift from the goal, then say "done" without checking.

The fix: a fixed objective on one side, a moving force of planning and action on the other. Each checks the other.

The result: an agent that cannot claim an outcome nobody confirmed.

An intellectual heritage

Ancient questions. Modern engineering.

Seven Indian schools of thought shaped the questions behind Sutra: knowledge, proof, action, truth.

  • वेदान्तVedanta

    What is real, what is the self, and how does the changing relate to the unchanging?

  • न्यायNyaya

    Systematic reasoning: valid knowledge, inference, evidence, and the examination of claims.

  • योगYoga

    Discipline, concentration, self-regulation and purposeful action.

  • सांख्यSankhya

    The distinction between consciousness and material processes. Offered as inspiration, not as a technical equivalent.

  • मीमांसाMimamsa

    Interpretation, context, duty, and the relationship between an instruction and the action it calls for.

  • प्रमाणBuddhist pramana traditions

    Rigorous study of perception, inference and cognition, and of when knowledge is justified.

  • अनेकान्तवादAnekantavada · Jain

    Many perspectives, and intellectual humility when a claim is complex or disputed.

  • Knowledgeandevidence
  • Reasoningandverification
  • Disciplineandcontrolled execution
  • The observerandthe record of what happened
  • Many perspectivesandstated uncertainty

These traditions do not literally describe modern AI, software architecture or autonomous agents. They are intellectual inspiration: questions that can inform engineering. Sutra's architecture is an original contemporary engineering project, and its implementation, algorithms, tests and performance stand on their own technical evidence.

02 · Intelligence architecture

Wrap any model in control.

SUTRA is not a foundation model and not a chatbot. It sits around any model and its tools: it decides what may be done, records what actually happened, and defines what counts as finished.

  1. Stillness the centreThe fixed objective, its success criteria and the rules that must remain true. Changed only by the user.
  2. Witness recordsWhat tools actually did, recorded outside the model’s own account of itself.
  3. Energy planning and controlPlanning, bounded retries, adaptation and recovery. This ring moves; the centre does not.
  4. Instruments models and toolsAny language model, any tool. Replaceable without changing the layers inside.

03 · Core capabilities

Six guarantees. No blind trust.

  • Keep the objective fixed

    Checks work against the original goal and flags drift. Only the user may amend it.

  • Gate consequential actions

    Classifies each action by kind and scope. Unknown actions fail closed and wait for approval.

  • Ground facts in records

    Treats tool records, not model statements, as the source for what happened. Altered records are detectable.

  • Separate doing from verifying

    Distinguishes attempted, completed and verified. Verified requires an independent check.

  • Plan with dependencies

    Breaks work into dependent steps, rejects impossible plans and limits re-planning.

  • Recover within bounds

    Names the type of failure, avoids repeating identical attempts, and escalates to a person when it should.

06 · Applications across AI systems

Works with any model. Any tool.

Because the control layer sits around the model, it is intended to serve many kinds of product.

  • Coding agents that edit files and run commands
  • Workflow and operations automation with approvals
  • Research and document pipelines that must show sources
  • Multi-model systems where models and tools are swapped over time
  • Any agent whose reports must be checked against what was done
